        👋 Welcome

        This is our private tool to track the family's investments and goals. Nothing is sent anywhere — all our numbers stay inside this browser.

        🥇 Golden Rules

        1. Your data lives only in this browser.
        2. Export a backup regularly to Google Drive.
        3. Update prices weekly.

        ✏️ How to update our numbers

        1. Tap the ⚙️ Settings tab.
        2. Under Holdings, find the fund's card and type the new Current price.
        3. Update the FX rate field when the dollar moves.
        4. To add a new holding, fill the Add Holding form and press Add Holding.
        5. After changing goals, press Save Profile.

        📅 How often to update what

        ItemHow often
        PricesWeekly
        DividendsQuarterly
        FX rateWeekly
        Invested amountOnly when you buy more
        Goals / profileRarely

        🔔 The notification bell

        🟢 fresh   🟡 due soon   🔴 overdue. The number on the bell = how many items are overdue.

        💾 Backing up (important)

        1. ⚙️ Settings → Export JSON.
        2. Save the file to your Google Drive folder 'Portfolio Backups'.
        3. To restore, use Import JSON and pick your latest file.

        📱 Moving to a new phone or computer

        Open the page, go to Settings → Import JSON, and choose the latest backup.

        📖 What the numbers mean

        • Coast FIRE — the amount that can grow to retirement without new money.
        • Total Invested — money we put in.
        • Net Annual Income — dividends after tax we can spend.
        • Unrealized Gain — paper profit.
        • Real Return — growth after inflation.
        • Semiconductor exposure — how much is in chip companies.
        • Buckets — Growth / Income / Stability.

        🧮 How the math works

        • Coast FIRE = future need ÷ growth.
        • Target price = dividend after tax ÷ desired yield.
        • Capital to add = income gap ÷ net yield.

        🛠️ If something looks wrong

        • Numbers zero? Press Save.
        • Chart missing? Refresh the page.
        • Lost data? Import your backup.

        🔒 Privacy

        Data stays in this browser; the website only hosts the code.
