        🚀 Welcome to Your Freedom Dashboard

        This is not a stock tracker. This is your command center for financial freedom.

        Every peso deployed today is a future employee working for you. The goal is simple: build assets that eventually buy back your time.

        🎯 The Mission

        The dashboard is built around a long-term freedom strategy:

        • Growth Bucket → builds long-term wealth and drives Coast FIRE.
        • Income Bucket → generates cash flow that reduces future expenses.
        • Stability Bucket → protects capital and funds family goals such as education.
        • Insurance Bucket → optional, illiquid assets that can be included when desired.

        The objective is not simply becoming wealthy. The objective is becoming free.

        🏝 Understanding Coast FIRE

        Coast FIRE means reaching a portfolio level that can grow into your future retirement target even if you stop making additional contributions.

        In this dashboard:

        • Growth assets count toward Coast FIRE.
        • Stability assets count toward Coast FIRE.
        • Income assets do not count as Coast FIRE capital.
        • Income assets instead reduce the expenses your portfolio must fund.
        • Insurance assets can optionally be included when modeled.

        Coast FIRE is where work starts becoming optional. Full FIRE is where work becomes a choice.

        📊 Reading the Dashboard

        • Wealth Capacity Now
          Shows how much of your annual spending is currently supported by your assets and income streams.
        • Full FIRE End Goal
          Tracks progress toward your long-term independence target.
        • Option to Slow Down
          Estimates when your current path reaches Coast FIRE.
        • Income-FI Progress
          Tracks progress toward your target income coverage percentage.
        • Allocation Charts
          Reveal where your capital is deployed and whether it aligns with your strategy.

        🧭 Coast Projector Explained

        The Coast Projector is the planning engine of the entire dashboard.

        It uses the same assumptions shown elsewhere in the application:

        • Current portfolio value
        • Bucket return assumptions
        • Monthly contributions
        • Annual contribution step-ups
        • Income coverage goals
        • Retirement spending targets

        The graph is the source of truth. All outputs beneath the graph are generated from the exact same model.

        The stacked chart tells the full wealth-building story:

        • Starting Portfolio
        • + Accumulated Deposits
        • + Unrealized Gains
        • = Portfolio Value

        The dashed line represents your Coast FIRE target.

        When the portfolio line crosses that target, you have achieved Coast FIRE under the selected assumptions.

        ⚙️ Contribution Profiles

        Your Default Distribution Profile defines where each monthly contribution goes.

        Example:

        • Growth = 70%
        • Income = 20%
        • Stability = 10%

        If the Coast Projector is configured to use only Growth, only the Growth portion of future contributions will be counted.

        The selected buckets determine:

        • Starting portfolio used by the model
        • Contribution share used by the model
        • Blended return assumptions used by the model

        💵 Income-FI Philosophy

        Income is not just cash flow. Income is freedom purchased in advance.

        Every peso generated by the Income Bucket is one less peso your retirement portfolio must support.

        Income coverage represents a future goal:

        • 25% = portfolio contributes meaningfully
        • 50% = lifestyle is partially self-funded
        • 100% = work becomes completely optional
