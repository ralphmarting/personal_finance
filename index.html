        👋 Welcome

        This dashboard tracks portfolio progress, income coverage, allocation, and long-term planning using data stored in your browser.

        🛠 Wealth Builder Best Practices

        1. Keep prices and dividends reasonably current.
        2. Export a backup regularly.
        3. Review goals when contribution plans or spending assumptions change.

        ✏️ Updating data

        1. Open Settings.
        2. Update holdings, prices, FX, or goal assumptions.
        3. Save your profile to refresh dashboard metrics and tools.

        🎯 Your Freedom Dashboard Explained

        • Coast FIRE Progress tracks progress toward a capital level that could grow into the long-term retirement target without additional contributions.
        • Income Coverage tracks portfolio dividend income relative to the configured coverage target, while also showing annual income against total annual spending.
        • Total Invested shows cumulative capital contributed.
        • Allocation charts show how portfolio value is distributed by bucket and by holding.

        💵 Income notes

        Income views focus on spendable dividends after tax. Auto-reinvested products can remain in the portfolio but are not counted in income calculations.

        💾 Backups

        Use Export JSON before major edits. To restore data on another device, open the dashboard and use Import JSON.

        🔒 Privacy

        Data stays in this browser unless you export it yourself. This dashboard is for personal planning and record keeping.
