## ✨ What you can do

- 📊 **See the big picture** — portfolio value, allocation by bucket and holding, income estimates, and saved snapshots.
- 🧺 **Organize your holdings** — track Growth, Income, Stability, and Insurance assets in PHP or USD.
- 💸 **Plan for income** — explore dividend income with the Dividend Planner and tune allocations with the Rebalancer.
- 🏝️ **Project your Coast FIRE journey** — model contribution plans and see how your assumptions affect the timeline.
- ✍️ **Keep things current** — record trades, update holding values, and celebrate milestones.
- 🌗 **Make it yours** — switch light/dark themes or use Share Mode to mask figures on screen.
- 💾 **Stay portable** — export and import JSON backups to move your data between browsers or devices.

## 🚀 Get started

PerFi is a static web app—no build step or package installation needed.

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open `index.html` in a modern browser with an internet connection.
3. Head to **Settings** and replace the preloaded example profile, holdings, and snapshots with your own.
4. Update your portfolio as needed, and export a JSON backup from **Settings → Backup & Restore**.

> ⚠️ **Fresh start tip:** PerFi begins with example data so you can explore the dashboard. Replace it with your own information before relying on its figures.

PerFi loads Chart.js from jsDelivr and the Manrope font from Google Fonts, so those resources need to be reachable for the full presentation.

## 🔐 Data & privacy

Your portfolio data stays in your browser's local storage. PerFi has no backend and does not send portfolio data to the project owner. Your browser does request the static Chart.js and font resources mentioned above; those services may receive standard request information.

Data is specific to the browser profile and site origin where you use PerFi. Export a JSON backup to keep a copy or move your data to another browser or device. Exported files contain your portfolio information, so store them carefully. **Share Mode only masks figures on screen**—it does not remove them from stored or exported data.

## ⚠️ A note on financial projections

PerFi is a personal tracking and planning tool, not financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. Projections are estimates based on the profile values and assumptions you enter; actual results may differ. Verify your assumptions and consult a qualified professional before making financial decisions.
